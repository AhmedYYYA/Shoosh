Editorial promise

Shoosh remains the source of truth.

The journal does not invent ratings or rewrite an experience into something more flattering. It preserves what was tried, what was paid at the time, practical booking guidance, and Shoosh’s qualitative observation.

Supporting venue imagery is clearly separated and credited; it never replaces Shoosh’s original experience image.
